# Lore and creative notes

This folder is yours. Write in whatever shape helps you think; nothing here is read by the game. When something becomes data (a villager, a quest, an item), it moves into `content/` in the formats under `docs/content/`.

Suggested starting files, with loose templates:

## towns/<town>.md

```
# Aboridge
One line: what this town is known for.
Industry / what they trade
Who runs it and how they feel about the war
How they see the demigod (and what label/tags would change that)
Key places (each becomes a map): main street, the keep, the market...
Villagers (recruitable): name, profession, romanceable?, one-line hook
Non-recruitables: name, role
Quests this town offers
What corruption has done here
```

## story/outline.md

```
Act 0: the fall, foraging, first fight, the messenger, arrival
Act 1: founding Withergate, meeting the towns, domain lock-in
Act 2: the discord between the cities, incursions
Act 3: the source of the corruption
Ascension and epilogues by domain and by relationships
For each beat: where it happens, who is involved, what the player chooses, what changes
```

## domains.md

```
For each domain: display name, what it stands for, how NPCs talk about it,
9 power ideas (3 tiers), who is drawn to it and who is repelled.
```

## items.md · enemies.md · facilities.md

Lists in plain prose first; I turn them into YAML with you once the shape settles.
